What the dataset had

Hidden missing values: isnull() shows zero nulls, but director has 2,588 rows and country has 287 rows filled with the text “Not Given”. I turned these into real NaN and then labelled them “Unknown”, because dropping them would lose about 29% of the rows.
Duplicates: there were 3 obvious ones with different show_ids (9-Feb, 15-Aug, 22-Jul). A fourth was hiding behind trailing whitespace in “Consequences “. It only showed up after I stripped the text columns, so 4 rows were removed in total (8,790 → 8,786).
Formats: date_added was stored as text. duration mixed minutes and seasons in one column. country and listed_in held several comma-separated values per cell.

Transformations

Dates: date_added is now a proper datetime, with year, month, weekday, quarter and years_to_add columns.
Duration: split into duration_min for movies and seasons for TV shows.
Country and genre: primary_country, n_countries, primary_genre and n_genres, plus 0/1 flags for the top 10 genres.
Ratings: the 14 rating codes are grouped into an ordered audience column (Kids, Older Kids, Teens, Adults, Not Rated).
Encoding: is_movie as a 0/1 label, with audience one-hot columns also built in the notebook. The exported CSV has 34 columns, 0 nulls (apart from the movie/TV-specific duration columns) and 0 duplicates.
