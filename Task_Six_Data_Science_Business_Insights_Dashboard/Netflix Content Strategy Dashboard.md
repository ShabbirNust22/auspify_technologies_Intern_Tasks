## Key recommendations

- Plan for a flat-to-modest band of about 1,700–2,400 additions a year; volume has plateaued.
- Keep investing in Japan, South Korea and France; review India, whose additions fell 15%.
- 67% of series have a single season: use viewing data to guide renewals.
- Use the audience model as a triage tool with human review for unrated titles.
